# IDDA Operator design system

Source of truth: DESIGN-airtable.md. Workflow: ui-ux-pro-max.
Scope: operator workspace only; passenger and official source explorer retain their design.

## Visual decisions
- White canvas #ffffff; ink/primary #181d26; body #333840; muted #41454d.
- Hairline #dddddd; soft #f8fafc; strong #e0e2e6; focus #458fff.
- Full-card signature surfaces: forest #0a2e0e, coral #aa2d00, cream #f5e9d4, peach #fcab79.
- Dark/primary surfaces use white text; cream/peach surfaces use ink.
- No gradients, decorative shadows, oversized bold headings or pricing-style pill buttons.
- Inter/system substitutes licensed Haas; display 40px/400, section 24px/400, body 14px/400.
- Radius: 12px primary/signature, 10px panels, 6px inputs; icon controls 44px circular.
- 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/96 spacing; 1280px max content and 48px desktop gutters.
- 64px white top navigation, editorial heading, metric surfaces, map/route rail, charts and alerts.
- Use 96px between major analytics bands; compact spacing inside operational controls.

## Interaction and truthfulness
- Working section navigation, refresh, mobile native menu and source disclosure.
- Preserve route selection, surge start/stop, map/GPS, history, forecast and recommendations.
- Keep simulator telemetry distinct from official daily validation counts and source dates.
- Styled, labeled native statistical filters with visible focus, loading and retry states.
- Accessible chart data tables; flat fills, dashed forecast, labels accompanying density colors.
- Mobile stacks panels without page overflow; reduced motion and keyboard controls supported.
- Generic skill palette/font/marketing CTA suggestions were discarded in favor of the source.
